Dystopia Connect Publisher

A personal tool that uploads my own animated YouTube Shorts to my own YouTube channel, Dystopia Connect.

What it does. When one of my animated Shorts is finished, this tool checks the video file and uploads it to my YouTube channel with its title, description, hashtags and thumbnail. It runs on my own computer, about once a day.

Who it is for. Only me, the owner of the Dystopia Connect channel. It is not a public service, and nobody else can sign in to it or use it.

How it uses YouTube

The tool uses the official YouTube Data API through Google sign-in (OAuth 2.0). It asks for permission to upload videos to my channel and to read my channel's basic details, so it can confirm each upload worked. It does not read, change or delete anything else.

Your data

The tool does not collect any information about viewers or other people. See the privacy policy.

Terms
